**Meta Description:** An honest comparison: how LeadVault's fresh, on-request data sourcing differs from database platforms like Apollo, ZoomInfo, and RocketReach — and when each approach fits.

## Header

### An Honest Comparison: The Warehouse vs. The Harvest.

Apollo. ZoomInfo. RocketReach. Lusha. These are serious companies with massive databases, and for some use cases they're a reasonable choice. This page explains — factually — how their model differs from ours, so you can decide what your business actually needs.

## The Two Models

**The Database Platform Model:** Build an enormous database once; verify it periodically; sell subscriptions to search and download it. Strength: instant self-serve access at scale. Structural weakness: **records age between verification cycles.** B2B data decays at roughly 25–30% per year as people change roles and companies — a figure the platforms themselves acknowledge in their own research. Users pay for access to records of mixed and unknowable freshness.

**The LeadVault Model:** Source on request. Your order defines the specification; our researchers and custom tools gather and verify records **for that order**, within days of delivery. Strength: freshness, precision, and a human strategist attached to every engagement. Trade-off: delivery takes days, not seconds — freshness cannot be instant.

## Side by Side

| Factor | Database Platforms | LeadVault |
|---|---|---|
| Access model | Self-serve login & search | Sourced on request, delivered to you |
| Data age at delivery | Unknown — verified anywhere from weeks to years ago | Days — verified for your order |
| Bounce handling | Bounces included in what you export | Bounces removed before delivery |
| Customization | Limited to database filters | Any specification we can define, we can source |
| Human guidance | None — software subscription | Strategist consultation with every engagement |
| African & emerging-market coverage | Thin and frequently outdated | Deep, current, on-the-ground capability |
| Pricing model | Per-seat subscriptions (commonly $600–$1,800+/yr per user; enterprise contracts far higher) | Pay per dataset or flexible monthly plans |
| Best for | Large teams needing instant self-serve volume | Businesses that need data that's **right**, fresh, and guided |

## When to Use Them — and When to Use Us

**Choose a database platform if:** you need thousands of self-serve lookups daily and your team can tolerate bounce rates and stale records as a cost of speed.

**Choose LeadVault if:** response rates matter more than instant access; you need markets the platforms cover poorly; you want a specification they can't filter for; your sender reputation can't afford bounces; or you want a human expert making sure you buy the *right* data.

Many of our clients use both — the platform for bulk convenience, LeadVault when the campaign actually has to work.

## The Bottom Line

Nobody in this market combines what we combine: **on-request fresh sourcing, per-order verification, custom engineering, deep emerging-market reach, and strategist service.** That combination — not any single feature — is why clients who try us, stay.
